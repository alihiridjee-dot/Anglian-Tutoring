## 7. Question figures (PR #258): how to finish it

**What it is.** Ali approved this on 17 Oct: "code draws graphs, bar charts and reaction profiles, and DeepSeek draws circuits and food webs, checked before they're saved". The practice queue's writer (Sonnet 5.5) may give up to 2 questions per quiz or task a figure brief, as JSON text in a `figure` field. The worker draws each one before saving. Code draws graphs, bar charts and profiles (`src/lib/figures/figureDraw.ts`). DeepSeek draws circuits and food webs (`drawFigure.server.ts`), with one retry and a deadline of job start + 280 s. Everything passes `figureCheck.ts`. A question whose figure fails is left out, never saved without it. Only the practice queue asks for figures: a tutor's builder drafts and "Replace questions" stay text only.
